## 💾 Database

### Automatic Setup
- SQLite database created automatically
- Two tables: `transactions` and `settings`
- Stored locally in user's AppData directory
- No external connections
- 100% private and offline

### What's Stored
- Transactions: date, type, category, amount, payment mode, description
- Settings: business name, owner name, currency
